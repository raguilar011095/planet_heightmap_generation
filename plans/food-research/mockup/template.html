World Orogen · Evolution · Food tab

The Food tab, rebuilt around its inputs

Every proposed view and its key, live on a small made-up planet, now with a Crops tab: each hearth’s own variants, which crops compete and which stack. This round covers what later history and culture will build on: what the land offers, and how farming began and spread over ten thousand years. Cultures, cooking, trade and cities are left for later.

  • Soil and Season fold into Farmland. All of it shapes People later.
  • Views show what came. “Best possible” shows the land alone.
  • 10,000 years by default, with the full spread one click away. Each place starts at its own time, and faster after its first crop.
  • A landmass may lack a beast. “Fewer gaps” makes that rarer.
  • Tree & palm crops join as a new food (my call: see Staple food).
  • Calendar first. The names are placeholders.

The planet’s climate here is a quick stand-in, so the page builds in about a second in your browser. The crop model is the app’s own, ported, with tree crops added. Shapes and patterns are what to judge; numbers will differ in Orogen. Hover or tap the map for the readout.

How farming began and spread

  • Wild ancestors placed by their own niches
  • Hearths start at dates drawn from their odds
  • Faster domestication once a place farms
  • Crops and beasts spread in years; places farming reaches first adopt
  • Each landmass’s beasts are luck
  • Each hearth grows its own variants; staples compete for a field and a season, the other crops stack

Building it, one phase at a time

  5. Crops & variantsEach hearth’s variants competing for the fields; pulses, orchards, spices and gardens stacking. Uses the crops research parked on branch crops.
